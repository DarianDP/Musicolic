Mr. Morale & The Big Steppers
Kendrick Lamar
★ ★ ★ ★ ★

This shit just keeps getting better

I think it's funny that people didn't like DAMN. for sounding too mainstream, and then people didn't like this for sounding too experimental. Which despite how different this is from DAMN. they're both definitely his most introspective and personal albums. But that's not why anyone dislikes this album. They dislike the sound of it. The sound of this is fucking incredible and I can only hope that he continues making albums in this style. He's always been incredibly experimental so this just feels like a natural next step. Also last thing. I feel like this is easily his most misunderstood album. I've seen people completely misunderstand the meanings of Worldwide Steppers, Auntie Diaries, and Mirror. The complaint that the album's too self indulgent is stupid. I'm gonna end this review with a quote from my sibling... that they said on discord but still ☠ "Kendrick stopped making music about political issues and used it as something therapeutic. People are bitching that he changed his style but he needed this and I know a lot of people need it. Mfs saying he's ignorant gotta be going through the same self indulgence Kendrick is defeating in Mr Morale."

DeBÍ TiRAR MáS FOToS
Bad Bunny
★ ★ ★ ★ ★

Not gonna write an expansive review here for the new Bad Bunny album as I think there are plenty of folks on here that can better speak to this album and its ties back to his home of Puerto Rico. But what I will say is that I really enjoyed this record from Bad Bunny and I think that has a lot to do with him expanding the sound here to include more salsa, jibaro and bachata tracks in addition to the reggaeton tracks he is famous for. Of course there are still plenty of reggaeton tracks here as that is his bread and butter, but it’s tracks like TURiSTA, CAFé CON RON, LO QUE LE PASO A HAWAIi, and BAILE INOLViIABLE that really felt fresh for him and had great instrumentation with bongos, horns, spanish guitars that he fit over quite nicely. I like him expanding his sound here and exploring all the different and beautiful sounds that latin music offers. Hope he does more of that moving forward. Favorite Tracks: NUEVAYol, BAILE INOLViIABLE, DtMF, LO QUE LE PASO A HAWAIi, CAFé CON RON, TURiSTA, PIToRRo DE COCO

DATA
Tainy
★ ★ ★ ★ ★

SIMPLEMENTE LA CABRA

Data es el primer álbum de Tainy, el mejor productor de la historia del género urbano. En este proyecto se luce, junta a varios de los mejores artistas tanto de la actualidad como del pasado, también artistas emergentes y algunos menos conocidos. Todos los temas tienen una producción exquisita, están cuidados al detalle y es una macedonia de sonidos que no se hacen nada repetitivos ya que toca bastantes estilos en el álbum. Sin duda es una representación de lo mejor del este género y para muchos es el mejor álbum de la historia. Mis favoritas son "Fantasma AVC", "Paranormal", "Si preguntan por mi" y "11 y Once".
