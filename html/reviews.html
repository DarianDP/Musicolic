Octane
Don Toliver
★ ★ ★ ★ ★

Hardstone Psycho was way better

Gave ‘Octane’ two full listens already, and I’m not gonna lie, I’m just not feeling it. This was one of my most anticipated releases of 2026, as I am a Don Toliver fan and from the rollout I expected it to be a different and experimental album, while it turned out to be the same generic rnb-like song over and over. Started off pretty strong with the first 2 tracks - E85 was heat and I loved Body’s production, it was just a super fun listen. On the third track, I heard Yeat at the beginning and got so hyped, was prepared to hear the potential “song of the year”, but I found it a generic af track plus Yeat’s part wasn’t all that. Features lowkey disappointed in general, except for Sahbabii’s part on K9 - this dude genuinely carried this song, made it one of my favourites here, but yeah.. Travis’ feature was disappointing to me, I didn’t quite enjoy Rema’s verse on ‘Secondhand’ too. I’d say the second half of the album was slightly better to me with tracks such as Gemstone, K9.. and the track Call Back finally releasing. But I just still find some of the songs there forgettable already. His previous release, Hardstone Psycho is miles ahead of Octane in my opinion. A way more exciting listen, way more replay value, production there was on a whole other level and the hype around it was something else. Even own it on CD. I’ll try listening to ‘Octane’ a few more times over the next days and see if my thoughts change. Favourite songs: Gemstone, Tiramisu, K9, Call Back, E85

Thriller
Michael Jackson
★ ★ ★ ★ ★

YEAH THIS ALBUM STILL GOATED

Note: This review is a reflection of my thoughts on this album’s artistic merit, not the artist himself as an individual. I’m just making my way through all the big popular albums on the app. But in revisiting Thriller, this was a project that I honestly cannot find unlikeable. It has something for everyone, whether you want to get down on the dance floor or simp to the some of ballads. The instrumentals are punchy, crisp and mixed incredibly well, you can listen to this project on a Fisher Price Chatter Phone and it would still sound pristine. Michael Jackson’s charisma and presence as a performer is undeniable at this point. Every track features some of the best vocal performances I’ve ever heard. Mike comes off as feisty, impassioned and edgy on here. The fact that every track on this album is a smash hit but also being quality in the way of songwriting, production and execution make this one of the best pop records in music history. Favorite tracks: Wanna Be Startin’ Somethin’, Beat It, Human Nature, PYT, The Lady in My Life

OUSI MAS OUSI QUE NUNCA

Omar Courtz presenta aqui, a mi parecer, uno de los mejores albumes del genero. Por debajo claro de DTMF, Sayonara o DATA, pero de los mejores sin duda. Hoy en día siento que el amor es mas egoista que nunca, uno ama en base a cuanto amor recibe del otro, pero el verdadero y mayor acto de amor es el amar incluso cuando la otra persona no nos aprecia del mismo modo. Lo triste es cuando ese sentimiento causa culpa en la otra persona porque a su modo de ver es necesario amar en cuanto ambos lo hagan en la misma medida, ousi lo sabe y quiere que sepa que por si mañana no esta, nunca negara la marca que dejo aquella persona en el y espera aunque no haber sido su todo, ser una parte especial que recuerde cuando conozco a aquel hombre que si la llene.
